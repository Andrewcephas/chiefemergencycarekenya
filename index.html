About Chief Emergency Care Kenya

Kenyan-based private medical training and emergency response organisation serving institutions, schools, county governments, and communities nationwide.

Chief Emergency Care-Kenya (CECK) is led by certified, experienced health professionals and instructors. We deliver rapid on-site emergency care and equip individuals and organisations with world-class, life-saving competencies through American Heart Association (AHA) accredited courses. Our mission is centered on uncompromising quality, prompt response, and measurable life-saving impact.

Our Services

Comprehensive emergency response, medical standby cover, and specialised healthcare capacity building.

Emergency Care & Event Medical Cover

Rapidly deployed first responders, certified EMTs, fully stocked trauma kits, and ambulances for sports tournaments, corporate events, conferences, and mass gatherings.

Disaster Management Partnerships

Collaborating with local authorities, NGOs, and corporations to formulate contingency response frameworks and manage mass-casualty scenarios effectively.

Clinical Placements & Mentorship

Hands-on clinical attachments and supervised trauma/emergency field placements tailored for nursing, medical, and clinical medicine students.

Corporate & Workplace Safety

OSHA-compliant workplace first aid training, routine safety audits, AED installation, and certified medical first-aid cabinet restocking for businesses.

Community & County Health Initiatives

Empowering community health promoters (CHPs), public clinic nurses, and first responders with subsidized accredited resuscitation certifications.

School & Youth Safety Programmes

Child CPR, choking intervention, sports injury care, and modular safety education customized for school teachers, sports coaches, and students.

AHA-Accredited Courses & Certifications

Internationally recognized life support programs delivered by licensed AHA instructors with official certification upon completion.

AHA Certified

First Aid, CPR & AED

Master the fundamentals of prompt bleeding control, fracture stabilization, burn care, and automated external defibrillator (AED) operation for adults, children, and infants.

AHA Healthcare

Basic Life Support (BLS)

Core resuscitation protocol training for doctors, nurses, paramedics, and allied healthcare practitioners emphasizing high-quality CPR and multi-rescuer team dynamics.

Advanced Clinical

ACLS (Adv. Cardiovascular Life Support)

Advanced diagnostic and pharmacology interventions for cardiopulmonary arrest, acute coronary syndromes, and life-threatening dysrhythmias.

Pediatric Focus

PALS (Pediatric Adv. Life Support)

Specialized assessment and systematic approach to managing critically ill pediatric patients experiencing respiratory distress or circulatory shock.

Specialized Home Care

Nanny & Caregiver Emergency Training

Comprehensive domestic child safety, infant CPR, choking relief, burn management, poisoning prevention, and emergency home readiness for nannies and parents.
